About

Preventive medicine
driven by AI

DrHouseGPT is an academic research project developed as part of the Master's in Computer Engineering at the University of Minho. It combines advanced NLP techniques with multiple medical data sources to build an intelligent assistant for preventive diagnosis.

The architecture is built on FastAPI, Streamlit, Ollama (local LLM) and a full RAG pipeline, with observability via Langfuse. The entire infrastructure runs in Docker containers for straightforward deployment.
